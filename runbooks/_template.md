## Trigger

## Triage

Questions to answer in the first few minutes, with the command or console location for each. End with the conditions under which to stop (false positive) or escalate.

## Contain

Numbered steps. Reversible actions first. Every command copy-pasteable, with `UPPER_SNAKE` placeholders. State the side effects of each step.

What to capture, how, and where to store it.

## Investigate

Queries and pivots to establish scope: what was accessed, what was changed, what persistence exists.

## Eradicate

How to remove the attacker's access and artifacts.

## Recover

How to return to normal operation and confirm it is safe.

## Follow up
